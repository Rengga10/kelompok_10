Observation Round handbag made of cable

About This Collaboration

In English class, we conducted observation activities on Round handbag made of cable and composed observation reports. Each team member provided a unique perspective in observing and analyzing the potential use of cable as the basic material for making Round handbag made of cable.

Team Observation Results

Decha

Observer & Analyst

Observation Round handbag made of cable

Electronic waste arises due to rapid technological advancement, which often leads to electronic devices or components becoming obsolete and simply being discarded. The same applies to unused cables that pile up in various places; these cluttered, unsightly heaps are a waste, as they could be put to better use. Therefore, we will create a round bag that utilizes these cables as both a decorative element and a container for small items..

The transformation begins by cutting long, discarded cables into 34cm segments. These segments are then combined with fabric to provide structure and strength, making it easier to form the desired shape. Instead of leaving them as an eyesore, we repurpose these cables into a functional new product..

The long cables are cut into 34cm segments, with the lengths gradually decreasing across four different sizes. These are then attached to the surface of a circular piece of fabric. The base is constructed from cables bonded together with adhesive, while a zipper is added to the top to close the bag. On the sides, there are attachments for a handle, making the bag easy to carry. It is essential to ensure that the adhesive is strong enough to keep the components secure so that the bag remains durable and items do not fall out.

The result is a unique bag made from cables, perfect for storing small items or serving as a charming fashion accessory. This transformation helps reduce electronic waste and encourages us to recycle waste whenever possible. Therefore, transforming waste cables into useful items is one way we can protect the Earth from damage—specifically, the degradation of soil quality (which can occur, for instance, when cables that take a long time to decompose are buried)

Observing Round handbag made of cable

Plastic waste is one of the biggest environmental problems in everyday life. Used cables, such as old charger cables and internet cables, are often thrown away when they are damaged. However, these cables still have strong and flexible plastic layers that can be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming used cables into a round handbag. This transformation shows how useless waste can become a fashionable and valuable product.

The transformation begins when used cables are collected and separated based on their color and size. The cables are then cleaned to remove dust and dirt. After that, the cables are cut and rolled to prepare them for weaving. At this stage, the original function of the cables changes. Instead of being used to carry electricity or data, they become the main material for a new bag.

The cleaned cables are combined with a zipper, a handle, and an inner lining to create a new object. Through the process of weaving and sewing the cables in a circle, the separate pieces form a strong round shape. Once the cables are firmly woven and the handle is attached, the separate components become one complete functional handbag.

As a result, used cables are transformed into a round handbag that has both functional and aesthetic value. The product can be used to carry small items while also serving as a unique fashion accessory with a colorful appearance. This transformation reduces plastic waste and supports a more sustainable environment.

Observing Round handbag made of cable

Cable waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology and electricity. Used cables are often thrown away when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these cables still have materials that can be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming used cables into a round bag. This transformation shows how cable waste can become a useful and valuable product.

The transformation begins when unused cables are prepared together with fabric, alteco glue, and other materials. The cables are attached to the fabric to form a circle using alteco glue. At this stage, the original function of the cables changes. Instead of being used to conduct electricity, they become decorative materials for a new product.

The cables are attached in circles, starting from the outer circle and continuing inward with a distance of about 1.5 to 2 cm, until 4 to 5 circles are formed. Through the process of gluing and sewing, the individual cables form a strong round side. The same side is made again for the back, and cardboard covered with fabric is attached inside to make it sturdy. The two sides are joined with a bottom side made of 6 cables, then a zipper, ring handle, and hook are added. Once all the parts are firmly attached, the separate cables become one complete bag.

As a result, used cable waste is transformed into a round bag that has both functional and aesthetic value. The product can be used to carry items while also serving as a unique fashion accessory. This transformation reduces the amount of cable waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling cable waste into bags is one way to support a more sustainable environment..
